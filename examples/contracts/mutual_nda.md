# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (sample)

1. Parties. This agreement is between Northwind Labs ("Company") and the counterparty named on the signature page ("Recipient").
2. Confidential Information. Any non-public business, technical or financial information disclosed in any form, including information disclosed orally that is not identified as confidential.
3. Term. This agreement lasts five (5) years from the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations survive for ten (10) years after termination.
4. Obligations. Recipient will use Confidential Information only to evaluate a potential business relationship and will protect it with at least reasonable care.
5. Exclusions. Information that is public, already known, independently developed, or received from a third party without restriction.
6. Non-solicitation. For three (3) years, Recipient will not solicit or hire any Company employee or contractor.
7. Remedies. Company may seek injunctive relief without posting bond. Recipient will pay Company's legal fees in any dispute, regardless of outcome.
8. Assignment. Company may assign this agreement without consent. Recipient may not assign it.
9. Governing law. Laws of the State of Delaware. Exclusive venue in Wilmington, Delaware.
10. Entire agreement. This is the entire agreement; changes must be in writing signed by both parties.
