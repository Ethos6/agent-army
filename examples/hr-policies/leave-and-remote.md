# Leave and remote work (sample HR policy)

Annual leave: full-time employees get 24 working days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year.
Sick leave: 10 paid days per year; a doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
Remote work: employees may work remotely up to 3 days a week; fully remote arrangements need director approval.
Equipment: the company provides a laptop and a 500 USD home-office stipend in the first year.
