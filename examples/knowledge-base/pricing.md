# Pricing (sample knowledge base)

Plans:
- Starter: free, up to 3 projects, community support.
- Pro: $29 per seat per month, unlimited projects, email support within one business day.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success manager and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Refunds: the Pro plan can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, cancellations stop
the next renewal but are not refunded. Enterprise refunds follow the signed order form.

Discounts: annual billing saves 20%. Registered non-profits and students get 50% off Pro.
